SEC 02

Eligibility

// who can enter

The Promotion is open worldwide to individuals who are at least eighteen (18) years of age at the time of entry, provided participation is permitted under applicable local laws and regulations.

SEC 03

Giveaway Period

// timing

The Promotion begins at 4:00 PM Mountain Standard Time (MST) on Tuesday, November 17, 2026, upon publication of the designated giveaway video on the 5H4N3 YouTube channel.

The Promotion will remain open for seven (7) calendar days and will close at 3:59 PM Mountain Standard Time (MST) on Tuesday, November 24, 2026 (the "Promotion Period").

For reference, the Promotion Period runs from 23:00 UTC on November 17, 2026 to 22:59 UTC on November 24, 2026. The Host's clock is the official timekeeping device for this Promotion.

Entries submitted after the closing deadline will not be eligible.

SEC 04

How to Enter

// entry protocol

To enter the Promotion:

  1. Visit the designated Neuroscape Metal Card Giveaway video on the 5H4N3 YouTube channel.
  2. Leave a comment answering the following question:
Entry question
"What is your favorite Neuroscape card and why?"
  1. Submit your comment before the Promotion Period ends.

No purchase, payment, or financial contribution of any kind is required to enter.

Limit one (1) entry per person. Multiple comments from the same individual will not increase their chances of winning. Automated entries, bots, or other fraudulent methods of entry are prohibited and will result in disqualification.

Subscribing to the 5H4N3 YouTube channel and liking the giveaway video are encouraged but are not required to enter.

All entries must comply with YouTube's Community Guidelines. Entries containing harassment, hate speech, spam, or other prohibited content may be disqualified.

SEC 06

Winner Selection and Notification

// the draw

One (1) potential winner will be selected through a random drawing from all eligible entries received during the Promotion Period.

The drawing will take place within seven (7) calendar days following the closing of the Promotion. The winner is expected to be announced in a video published on the 5H4N3 YouTube channel on or about Thursday, November 26, 2026.

The potential winner will be contacted through a reply to their original YouTube comment and/or another mutually agreed‑upon communication method.

The potential winner must respond within seven (7) calendar days of the initial notification to claim the prize and provide the information reasonably necessary to verify eligibility and arrange delivery.

If the potential winner does not respond within the specified period, is found to be ineligible, or declines the prize, the Sponsor and Host reserve the right to select an alternate winner through another random drawing.

The Sponsor and Host reserve the right to verify the eligibility of the selected winner before awarding the prize.

Odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received.

SEC 07

Privacy and Personal Information

// data handling

Personal information will only be requested from the selected winner and will be used solely for purposes related to administering this Promotion, verifying eligibility, and coordinating prize delivery.

Information that may be collected includes the winner's name, contact information, shipping address, and YouTube username.

This information may be shared between the Sponsor and Host, and with shipping providers when necessary to fulfill the prize.

Personal information will not be sold or used for unrelated marketing purposes without the winner's separate consent.

Personal information collected for this Promotion will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary to administer the giveaway and satisfy applicable legal obligations, after which it will be securely deleted, subject to applicable record‑retention requirements.
